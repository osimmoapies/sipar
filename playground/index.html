    What is real here. The interpreter is the released Sipar package, unchanged, running on Python in your browser (Pyodide). Labels, sinks, the allow list, secrets and the journal are the real code. Only the last step is recorded instead of performed: a command is not started, a request gets a canned answer. There is no real model: model.json plays the model, and you write it.
    
    
    What stays here. Everything runs on this page. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; a shared link keeps your edits in the part after #, which browsers do not send to a server.
    
    
    What it is built on. Python in the browser is Pyodide, served here unchanged from its release. Pyodide is under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 and its source is at that link. Sipar itself is under MIT or Apache-2.0, as you prefer.
